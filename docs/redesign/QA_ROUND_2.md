# QA Round 2: Copy & Messaging

*Phase 9 · read as a first-time visitor, 2026-09-11*

| Question | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Do I know what this is in 5 seconds? | Tagline explained the technology, not the use | Standfirst: find stocks that fit an idea, with three concrete examples, then "real SQL" and "daily-refreshed prices" |
| Do I trust the numbers? | Badges only | Badges + dateline + colophon naming the source (Yahoo Finance), refresh schedule, the snapshot trade-off, and "not investment advice" |
| Do I know what to do next? | Run button small; presets at the bottom of the sidebar | Primary "Run query" button; presets one tap away; empty states and errors give a next step; drawer actions lead to follow-up queries |
| Can a non-SQL visitor succeed? | Unexplained column names | Column reference in plain English; autocomplete; presets |
| Does it sound like the owner? | README voice was candid and exact | Kept: "snapshot, not a live feed", "nothing that can sleep, cold-start or go down" |

## Final adjustments made in this round

- Placeholders end in "…" and show an example ("e.g. 10…")
- Curly quotes and apostrophes in all visible copy
- Data badge shortened to "103 stocks · updated under an hour ago"
- Sort options use words ("highest first") instead of ▲▼

## Kept deliberately

- **Sentence case** for headings and buttons. The web-interface guidelines prefer Title Case, but sentence case matches the broadsheet voice and the rest of the copy.
